Elective · Chemistry 1 · Grade 11 Academic (Medical)

Chemistry in Daily Life

A mission with Dr. Otter

Learning Objectives

  • Give examples of chemistry applications in health, environment, and industry.
  • Explain how chemistry provides solutions to everyday problems in these fields.
  • Explain one way chemistry contributes to addressing a health, environmental, or industrial challenge using a clear real-world example.
  • Recognize career opportunities in chemistry-related fields.

Learning Competencies

  • Recall fundamental chemistry concepts such as matter, elements, compounds, and reactions.
  • Describe how chemistry contributes to solving real-world problems in health, environment, and industry as well as career opportunities in chemistry-related fields.
